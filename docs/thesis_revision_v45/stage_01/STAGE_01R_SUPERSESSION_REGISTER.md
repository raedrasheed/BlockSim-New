# Stage 1R — Supersession Register

Stage 1R supersedes specific Stage-1Q (and earlier) statements by RECORDING the supersession here and updating
ONLY the un-suffixed normative documents (`STAGE_01_*.md` / `STAGE_01_*.csv`). Historical Stage-1A…1Q lettered
artifacts (`STAGE_01A_*`…`STAGE_01Q_*`) are NOT modified — a superseded statement may still appear verbatim in a
frozen lettered artifact as a historical record. Name remains **PoCol**; the mechanism is **the idle policy within
PoCol**; the A1 baseline (`8.420833333 kWh`) is unchanged.
