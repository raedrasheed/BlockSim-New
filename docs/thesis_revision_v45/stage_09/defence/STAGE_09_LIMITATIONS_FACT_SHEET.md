# Stage 9 — Limitations Fact Sheet (defence)

- Reduced-scale confirmatory design (20 miners, 300 s, 1600-nonce domain, 12 paired seeds); simulator-only, no hardware measurement, assumed power values.
- Matched PoW control shares the template with PoCol: controlled comparison, not Bitcoin/real-world PoW; limited header diversity.
- Static contiguous range ownership under heterogeneous rates lengthens solution-discovery latency; instant restart from low-power listen is an assumption.
- Useful-work floor is an operational target, not a security guarantee: no common-prefix, chain-quality or incentive-compatibility proof.
- Both preregistered joint acceptance rules were not fully satisfied; results not generalisable beyond the evaluated configuration.
