# Stage 9 — Defence Brief

One-line thesis: PoCol is a coordinated, common-template variant of Proof-of-Work that eliminates exact-input duplication and reduces calculated energy through low-power-state residency, at a quantified energy/work-efficiency versus service/latency trade-off under the evaluated configuration.

Say: "under the evaluated configuration", "matched same-template PoW control", "calculated energy", "relative to the no-floor PoCol control", "within-run power-null reference".
Never say: PoCol beats PoW; PoW-equivalent performance/security; reduces Bitcoin energy; partitioning alone reduces energy; maintains 90% of PoW block production.

Three defensible claims: (1) duplicate-input elimination under the honest common template; (2) substantial calculated energy and work-efficiency gains; (3) an honest, preregistered trade-off with negative results and two NOT-LICENSED joint rules. Security and incentive properties remain future work.
